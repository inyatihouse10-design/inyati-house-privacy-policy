Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 September 2026

1. Who we are

Inyati House is a private self-catering accommodation property situated within Doornkop Fish & Wildlife Reserve, Mpumalanga, South Africa.

The property is owned by Cornish Properties and managed by Alan van der Vyver.

For privacy-related questions, requests or concerns, please contact info@inyati-africa.co.za.

2. What information we collect

When you enquire about staying at Inyati House, including through a Meta/Facebook or Instagram lead form, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your telephone or mobile number
  • Your email address
  • The month in which you would like to stay
  • The number of guests in your party
  • The dates you are considering for your stay
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide as part of your enquiry

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through our accommodation enquiry forms.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your accommodation enquiry
  • Check availability for your preferred dates
  • Provide information about rates, accommodation and booking arrangements
  • Communicate with you about your enquiry
  • Process and manage bookings where applicable
  • Respond to questions or requests you send to us
  • Provide information about relevant Inyati House offers or specials where we are permitted to do so

We only seek to collect information that is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

4. Marketing communications

If you provide consent to receive marketing communications from Inyati House, we may use your contact details to send occasional information about Inyati House, including accommodation specials, offers and opportunities to book a future stay.

You may withdraw your consent or request that marketing communications stop at any time by contacting info@inyati-africa.co.za.

Any marketing communication will identify the sender and provide an appropriate means of requesting that further marketing communications cease.

5. How your information is shared

Your personal information may be accessed by the people responsible for managing Inyati House enquiries and bookings, including the property management team and authorised marketing personnel.

Information may also be processed through service providers or technology platforms used to operate our enquiry, communication or booking processes, where reasonably necessary for those purposes.

We do not sell your personal information.

We will not disclose your personal information to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes without an appropriate lawful basis or your consent where required.

6. Meta lead forms

If you submit your information through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, the information you provide is collected through Meta's platform and made available to the authorised Inyati House representatives responsible for responding to accommodation enquiries.

Your information will be handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy once it is received by Inyati House.

Your use of Facebook or Instagram is also subject to Meta's own terms and privacy policies.

7. How we protect your information

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect the personal information we hold against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. However, reasonable safeguards will be applied having regard to the nature of the information and the purposes for which it is processed.

8. How long we keep your information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing bookings, maintaining appropriate business records and complying with applicable legal obligations.

Where information is no longer required, it may be securely deleted or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of information where there is a lawful basis to do so
  • Object to certain processing of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Object to or opt out of direct marketing communications

Requests can be made by contacting info@inyati-africa.co.za.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

10. Complaints

If you have a concern about how your personal information has been handled, we encourage you to contact us first so that we can try to resolve the matter.

You also have the right, where applicable, to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Information about the Information Regulator and its complaint processes is available through its official website.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements or the way we operate Inyati House.

The latest version will be made available through the privacy-policy link provided in our lead-generation and communication channels.
